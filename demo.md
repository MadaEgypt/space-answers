# Demo video

A two-minute walk through Space Answers for Confluence, recorded on our test site (staging install), Confluence Cloud, October 2026. No sound; captions are on screen.

<video src="demo.mp4" poster="demo-poster.jpg" controls preload="metadata" playsinline style="width:100%;max-width:960px;border-radius:8px"></video>

[Download the video (MP4, 1920×1080)](demo.mp4)

What it shows, in order:

1. A space admin enables Space Answers in a space and chooses who can ask and who can answer (**Space settings → Integrations → Space Answers**).
2. A member opens **Questions** in the space sidebar and asks. Members outside the answer group see who can answer.
3. The people who answer get the new question in the Confluence bell, open it and answer.
4. The person who asked gets the new answer in the bell and accepts it. Accepted answers are listed first.
5. Plain Confluence search finds the question by a word that appears only in the answer, and opens the question's Confluence page with all its answers.
